## What it does

Technician Coach AI reviews completed work orders, photos, and customer feedback to give every technician personalized coaching — raising first-time-fix rates and customer satisfaction without adding supervisors.

## Key features

- **Work-order review** — AI checks completeness, parts used, and time-on-task.
- **Photo quality checks** — before/after photos assessed against your standards.
- **Personal coaching cards** — one actionable tip per tech per week.
- **First-time-fix analytics** — FTR trends by tech, job type, and region.
- **Customer sentiment link** — reviews and CSAT tied back to individual jobs.

## Who it's for

Field-service, HVAC, telecom, and maintenance companies with 10+ technicians.
